# Bounded two-hour RL study

Started at the user's request on 2026-10-18. The request window began at
20:44:30 UTC and ended at **22:44:30 UTC**. Preparation and verification were part
of that window; the detached controller was launched at 20:52 UTC.

[Live results report](../experiments/20261018T205204367200Z_two_hour_rl_study_53593784/report.md)
and [live status](../experiments/20261018T205204367200Z_two_hour_rl_study_53593784/status.json)
are updated locally as jobs finish, with status refreshed during long jobs.

The prespecified plan includes:

- Full regression and lint gate before experiments.
- Fresh seeds 200–219 for the existing frozen checkpoint, uniform control, and
  untrained action-space control, with controls shared across training replicas.
- Five independent 40-episode training replicas with non-overlapping episode
  seeds; each is evaluated on at least the first five primary seeds if its jobs
  finish within the deadline.
- Explicit transfer tests on rectangle/sinusoidal bodies and Bi=0.1/10, using
  seeds 300–304 and the unchanged original checkpoint.
- Additional replica evaluations and a no-fallback ablation if time remains.

Jobs run sequentially to keep resource contention comparable. Not every planned
job is guaranteed to fit. The controller stops admitting jobs near the deadline,
terminates its own active worker at the hard limit, preserves partial progress,
and labels uncompleted work. A numerical/regression failure halts the study.
Failures and partial groups remain visible rather than being dropped from the
account of the experiment. Medians use completed evaluations only; paired
comparisons require complete pairs. No model is selected using held-out scores.

Each completed evaluation uses exactly 41 search PDE solves plus validation of
the same physical cell union at 200/400 resolution. Geometric constraints,
solver diagnostics, source protection, and area/perimeter budgets remain active.
Transferred policies deliberately run outside their original training condition;
these results are not pooled with in-distribution scores.

The reference model is copied into the experiment directory; `models/` is not
overwritten. Code, tests, configuration, environment and checkpoint hashes are
saved before launching, and workers import that frozen source copy. New replicas
and per-episode checkpoints stay inside their job folders.

The controller requests temporary Windows idle-sleep prevention while active,
then releases it. It does not change the permanent power plan. Creating a `STOP`
file in the study directory stops the worker and writes a partial report.

The live Markdown report is generated by the local controller and is available
without this chat turn remaining active. See `plan.json`, `jobs/`, `controller.log`,
and `source_manifest.json` in the experiment directory for detailed evidence.

## Completed results and next work

The controller stopped admitting work at 22:28:33 UTC because the estimated next
training job would not fit its remaining budget. It completed the regression
gate, four training replicas, and 46 evaluations. Four evaluation jobs failed:
the reference policy on seed 201 and replica 1 on seed 200 exhausted their 4100
proposal attempts (38/41 and 31/41 solves). Replica 3 seed 204 and replica 4 seed
202 encountered Windows access-denied errors replacing progress.json. These last
two are infrastructure failures, not evidence of a geometric feasibility failure.
The fifth training replica, transfer tests, later seed batches and no-fallback
ablation were not run. The original frozen report and failed-job files remain intact.

Reference RL wins 8/9 completed matched seeds against uniform; the untrained
control wins 8/10. Independent training replica paired win counts are 2/4,
4/5, 2/4, and 1/4. Unequal completion and infrastructure failures limit comparison.
These results do not establish a reliable learned advantage over the structured
untrained control, training stability, transfer performance, or wall-time speedup.
New completed training used 3363 PDE solves; evaluation used 1886, plus 184
refinement solves. Failed jobs consumed additional work. All 46 completed gains
survive the reported empirical grid-sensitivity criterion.

The current worker retries short file-replacement permission failures and keeps
permanent failures visible. Future controllers clear stale progress and idle-sleep
flags at exit. These changes do not alter the sealed study or retroactively mark
failed jobs successful. The next scientific milestone is the implemented
[deletion hypothesis audit](deletion_results.md), followed by sensitivity-guided
search with new seeds and all probing costs included.
